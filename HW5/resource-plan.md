# HW5 — Resource Plan

## 1. Project Overview

The Smart Parking Platform is a software-based solution designed to help drivers locate available parking spaces, view parking availability, and improve parking management efficiency. This resource plan identifies the personnel needed to design, develop, test, and deploy the platform.

For planning purposes, the project is estimated to take three months (12 weeks). All team members and compensation rates are fictional.

## 4. Resource Management Strategy

The Project Manager coordinates team activities, monitors progress, and ensures project milestones are achieved. The Software Developer is allocated the greatest number of hours because application development represents the largest portion of the project workload.

The UI/UX Designer concentrates on early-stage interface design, while the QA Tester focuses on validating features and identifying defects throughout development. The DevOps Engineer supports infrastructure setup, deployment, and monitoring.

Weekly progress meetings will be used to review assignments, identify resource constraints, and adjust workloads when necessary.

## 5. Assumptions

- The project duration is 12 weeks (three months).
- Each full-time team member has 40 available work hours per week.
- Team members are assigned part-time based on their allocation percentages.
- All names, hourly rates, and labor estimates are fictional.
- Infrastructure, software licenses, and contingency expenses will be included separately in the Cost Plan.
- The estimated total labor budget is $31,200.
