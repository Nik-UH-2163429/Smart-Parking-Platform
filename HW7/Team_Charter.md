# HW7 — Team Charter

## 1. Team Overview

**Project Name:** Smart Parking Platform  
**Document Type:** Team Charter  
**Project Methodology:** Agile / Scrum  
**Team Structure:** Fictional academic project team

The Smart Parking Platform is a software-based system designed to help drivers locate available parking spaces, view parking availability, and improve parking management efficiency.

This Team Charter establishes the expectations, responsibilities, communication practices, and operating procedures for the project team.

## 2. Team Mission

Our mission is to collaboratively design and develop a reliable, user-friendly Smart Parking Platform that improves the parking experience through accessible information and efficient parking management.

The team is committed to delivering quality work while maintaining accountability, communication, and professional collaboration throughout the project.

## 3. Team Goals and Objectives

- Develop a functional Smart Parking Platform that meets established project requirements.
- Follow Agile and Scrum principles to organize development activities.
- Complete project deliverables according to the approved schedule.
- Maintain clear and consistent communication among team members.
- Identify and resolve technical challenges before they significantly affect project progress.
- Conduct testing and quality assurance to ensure reliable system performance.
- Maintain accurate project documentation in the GitHub repository.

## 4. Team Roles and Responsibilities

| Team Member | Role | Primary Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| Alex Carter | Project Manager | Oversees scheduling, planning, risk management, resource allocation, and stakeholder communication. |
| Jordan Lee | Software Developer | Implements platform features, maintains source code, and resolves technical issues. |

Additional development, testing, and reporting responsibilities will be assigned according to the existing Resource Plan and RACI Matrix.

Each team member is expected to complete assigned tasks and communicate any obstacles that could prevent timely completion.

## 5. Leadership Approach

The project team will primarily use a **democratic leadership style**, allowing team members to contribute ideas and participate in decisions.

The Project Manager will encourage collaboration, gather feedback, and consider technical recommendations before making major project decisions.

A more authoritative approach may be used when urgent decisions are required, such as responding to critical system failures or significant scheduling risks.

Servant leadership principles will also be incorporated by supporting team members, addressing obstacles, and encouraging professional development.

## 6. Team Values

The project team will operate according to the following principles:

- **Accountability:** Every member is responsible for assigned tasks and deadlines.
- **Respect:** Team members will treat one another professionally.
- **Transparency:** Project progress, risks, and challenges will be communicated openly.
- **Collaboration:** Team members will share knowledge and support one another.
- **Quality:** Deliverables will be reviewed and tested before completion.
- **Continuous Improvement:** Feedback will be used to improve processes and project outcomes.

## 7. Communication Plan

| Communication Type | Method | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Team Progress Updates | Project meetings | Weekly |
| Task Management | GitHub project documentation | As needed |
| Technical Discussions | Team meetings or direct communication | As needed |
| Stakeholder Updates | Recorded project update videos | Weekly |
| Risk and Issue Reporting | Risk Register and project documentation | When identified |
| Sprint Review | Project progress review | End of each sprint |

Team members should communicate significant obstacles promptly rather than waiting until the next scheduled meeting.

## 8. Decision-Making Procedures

The team will follow a collaborative decision-making process:

1. Identify the issue or decision that needs to be addressed.
2. Gather relevant information and recommendations.
3. Discuss possible solutions with affected team members.
4. Evaluate alternatives based on project objectives, cost, risk, and schedule.
5. Allow the Project Manager to make the final decision when agreement cannot be reached.
6. Document significant decisions and communicate them to relevant stakeholders.

## 9. Conflict Resolution Procedures

Conflicts may arise from differing technical opinions, scheduling pressures, or resource limitations.

The team will primarily use **collaboration and compromise** to resolve disagreements.

The conflict resolution process will include:

1. Identify the source of the disagreement.
2. Allow each team member to explain their concerns.
3. Encourage respectful discussion without assigning blame.
4. Evaluate potential solutions based on project priorities.
5. Agree on an appropriate resolution and assign responsibilities.
6. Escalate unresolved conflicts to the Project Manager.

The goal is to resolve disagreements constructively while maintaining team productivity and trust.

## 10. Team Performance Expectations

Team performance will be evaluated using the following measures:

| Performance Area | Success Criteria |
|---|---|
| Schedule Management | Assigned milestones are completed by established deadlines. |
| Task Completion | Sprint backlog items are completed and documented. |
| Software Quality | Critical defects are resolved before release. |
| Communication | Progress and obstacles are communicated consistently. |
| Documentation | Project files remain accurate and organized in GitHub. |
| Collaboration | Team members participate constructively in project decisions. |

## 11. Risk and Issue Management

The team will use the existing project Risk Register to identify, evaluate, and respond to potential project threats.

Technical issues, resource limitations, and scheduling concerns will be reported to the Project Manager.

Significant risks will be assessed for probability and impact, and appropriate mitigation strategies will be documented.

## 12. Team Agreement

All fictional project team members are expected to follow the responsibilities and operating procedures described in this charter.

The Team Charter may be updated if project requirements, stakeholder expectations, or team responsibilities change.
