# Smart Parking Platform - Risk Register

The following risk register identifies major risks that could affect the development and implementation of the Smart Parking Platform.

| ID | Risk Description | Category | Probability | Impact | Owner | Response Strategy | Status / Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Parking availability data may not update accurately or in real time. | Technical | High | Major | Development Team | Mitigate | Test data updates and validate parking availability information regularly. |
| R2 | GPS or location services may provide inaccurate parking location information. | Technical | Moderate | Moderate | Development Team | Mitigate | Test location accuracy and provide users with alternative location selection options. |
| R3 | Integration with parking sensors or external data sources may fail. | Technical | Moderate | Major | Development Team | Mitigate | Test integrations early and maintain backup data options when possible. |
| R4 | The application may experience server downtime or poor performance during high usage. | Technical | Moderate | Major | Development Team | Mitigate | Perform load testing and monitor application performance. |
| R5 | Development tasks may take longer than originally estimated. | Schedule | High | Moderate | Project Manager | Mitigate | Track tasks weekly and adjust priorities when tasks fall behind. |
| R6 | External parking data integration may be delayed. | Schedule | Moderate | Major | Development Team | Mitigate | Begin integration early and use test/mock data during development. |
| R7 | Testing and debugging may take longer than planned. | Schedule | Moderate | Moderate | QA / Development Team | Mitigate | Test throughout development instead of waiting until the end of the project. |
| R8 | Hosting or cloud services may cost more than expected. | Financial | Moderate | Moderate | Project Manager | Mitigate | Monitor service usage and select low cost or free options when appropriate. |
| R9 | Parking sensors or other required hardware may exceed the planned budget. | Financial | Low | Major | Project Manager | Accept / Mitigate | Compare hardware alternatives and limit prototype hardware purchases. |
| R10 | A team member may become unavailable during an important project stage. | People | Moderate | Major | Project Manager | Mitigate | Document work and distribute project knowledge among multiple team members. |
| R11 | Differences in team members' technical experience may slow development. | People | Moderate | Moderate | Project Manager | Mitigate | Assign tasks based on skills and encourage team members to assist each other. |
| R12 | Poor team communication may result in duplicated, missed, or incomplete work. | People | Moderate | Major | Entire Team | Mitigate | Use regular meetings and GitHub updates to communicate task progress. |
