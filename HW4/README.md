# HW4 - Risk, Quality, and Communication Management

## Smart Parking Platform

In this assignment, we identify potential project risks for the Smart Parking Platform, develop a risk register, and establish a communication plan for managing the project.

## Risk Identification

### Technical Risks

1. Parking availability data may not update accurately or in real time.
2. GPS or location services may provide inaccurate parking location information.
3. Integration with parking sensors or external parking data sources may fail.
4. The application may experience server downtime or performance issues during periods of high usage.

### Schedule Risks

1. Development tasks may take longer than originally estimated.
2. Delays in integrating parking data could affect the project schedule.
3. Testing and debugging may reveal issues that delay planned milestones.
4. Team member availability may cause assigned tasks or sprint goals to fall behind schedule.

### Financial Risks

1. Cloud services may introduce unexpected costs.
2. Database expenses may increase as the number of users grows.
3. Parking sensor or other hardware costs could exceed the planned project budget.
4. Additional development or maintenance work may require resources beyond the original budget.

### People Risks

1. A team member may become unavailable during an important stage of development.
2. Team members may have different levels of experience with the technologies used in the project.
3. Poor communication between team members/groups could result in duplicated or incomplete work.
4. Disagreements about project priorities could slow development.
