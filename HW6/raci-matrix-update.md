## 2. RACI Definitions

RACI establishes how project team members participate in individual activities.

- **R – Responsible:** Performs the work required to complete the activity.
- **A – Accountable:** Ultimately owns the activity and approves the deliverable.
- **C – Consulted:** Provides advice, expertise, or feedback.
- **I – Informed:** Receives updates about progress or decisions.

Each activity must have exactly one Accountable team member and at least one Responsible team member.

A/R indicates that one team member performs both functions.

## 7. RACI Management Strategy

The project team will use the matrix to maintain clear responsibilities throughout the development lifecycle.

The following procedures will be followed:

1. Assign exactly one Accountable team member to each activity.
2. Assign at least one Responsible team member to each activity.
3. Consult supporting team members when their expertise is required.
4. Keep relevant stakeholders informed.
5. Review responsibility assignments during weekly project meetings.
6. Document changes to project roles or assignments through GitHub.
7. Update the matrix when requirements or project responsibilities change.
