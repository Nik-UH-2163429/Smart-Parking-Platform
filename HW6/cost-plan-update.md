## 2. Cost Estimation Method

The project continues to use the **bottom-up cost estimation method**.

Individual labor and operational expenses are estimated and combined to calculate the overall project budget.

**Estimated Labor Cost = Estimated Hours × Hourly Rate**

**Total Project Budget = Labor + Infrastructure + Tools + APIs + Contingency**

The budget assumes a 12-week development period with five fictional team members.

## 5. Contingency Reserve

The project retains its original 10% contingency reserve.

**Calculation:**

Labor: $31,200

Non-Labor Expenses: $950

Subtotal: $32,150

Contingency Reserve: $32,150 × 10% = $3,215

The contingency reserve is intended to address identified uncertainties including:

- Unexpected infrastructure expenses.
- Additional testing and debugging.
- API usage increases.
- Minor schedule delays.
- Development rework and integration issues.

5. Evaluate cost implications before approving changes.
6. Document budget revisions in GitHub.
7. Communicate significant budget concerns through stakeholder updates.

## 10. Budget Change Control

Proposed budget changes should be reviewed before implementation.

The review process will include:

- Identifying the activity requiring additional funding.
- Estimating additional labor or operational expenses.
- Assessing the effects on the overall project schedule.
- Determining whether existing resources can be reallocated.
- Evaluating whether contingency funds are appropriate.
- Recording approved budget revisions.

No additional spending is assumed solely because a new WBS task has been identified.
